How trading works

Cairn gets a quote from AVNU and checks the exact token approval, exchange, amounts and recipient. Your wallet signs and submits the swap. Cairn never receives your seed phrase or private key.

This first release supports STRK, ETH, native USDC, bridged USDC.e, USDT and WBTC. Buy means spending your payment token; Sell means spending the selected asset. Both are exact-input swaps.

Wallets, mobile & privacy

Use an installed Starknet wallet such as Ready or Braavos. On mobile, open this page in your wallet's dapp browser. If Telegram's browser cannot detect your wallet, use your external browser or open it in Braavos.

Your wallet address goes to AVNU and our configured Starknet RPC to build quotes and read balances. Trade history is kept only in this browser. No Telegram identity is linked to your wallet. Clearing local history does not delete public blockchain records.
